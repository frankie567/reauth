<div class="feature-grid">
<section><span class="feature-number">01 / Factors</span><h3>Start with identity.</h3><p>Email OTP, HOTP, TOTP, backup codes, and social login. The primitives behind your authentication flows.</p></section>
<section><span class="feature-number">02 / MFA</span><h3>Make every step count.</h3><p>Bring factors together in multi-factor workflows and track progress through authentication sessions.</p></section>
<section><span class="feature-number">03 / Sessions</span><h3>Keep the connection.</h3><p>Manage authenticated identity sessions with persistence that fits your application and your data storage.</p></section>
